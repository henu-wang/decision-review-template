A structured post-decision review template system for continuous improvement of your decision-making skills. While most people focus only on making decisions, the review phase is where the real learning happens. These templates help you systematically analyze past decisions to extract maximum insight and improve future judgment.

## The Power of Decision Reviews

Most organizations and individuals spend significant energy making decisions but almost no time reviewing them afterward. This is a massive missed opportunity. Post-decision reviews (also called retrospectives, after-action reviews, or decision audits) are one of the most effective ways to improve judgment over time.

Ray Dalio built Bridgewater Associates into the world's largest hedge fund by obsessively reviewing past decisions and encoding lessons into principles. Annie Duke, former professional poker player and decision strategist, advocates for a structured approach to separating decision quality from outcome quality. The key insight is that good decisions can have bad outcomes and bad decisions can have good outcomes; what matters for learning is the quality of the process.

Warren Buffett writes annual letters reviewing his investment decisions, including his mistakes. He credits this practice with helping him avoid repeating errors and continuously refining his investment criteria.

### 6. Failure Analysis Template (`templates/failure-analysis.md`)
Dedicated template for analyzing decisions that led to negative outcomes. Uses the "5 Whys" method and root cause analysis.

## Review Framework

Every review follows this evidence-based structure:

| Phase | Questions | Purpose |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Recall | What did I decide and why? | Reconstruct original thinking |
| Outcome | What actually happened? | Document results objectively |
| Process | Was my decision process sound? | Separate process from outcome |
| Comparison | How did reality differ from expectations? | Identify prediction errors |
| Counterfactual | What would have happened with other choices? | Evaluate alternatives |
| Principles | What rules or principles emerge? | Extract transferable lessons |
| Action | What will I do differently next time? | Commit to improvements |

## Key Metrics

Track these metrics across your decision reviews:

- **Calibration Score**: Do your confidence levels match actual outcomes?
- **Process Quality**: Rate 1-5 on criteria like information gathering, bias checking, and alternatives considered
- **Learning Velocity**: How quickly are you improving based on review insights?
- **Pattern Recognition**: What recurring decision errors do you make?

## How to Use

1. **At Decision Time**: Record your decision, reasoning, and confidence (use the Decision Journal Template)
2. **Set Review Date**: Schedule a review for 30-90 days after the decision
3. **Conduct Review**: Use the appropriate template when the outcome is known
4. **Extract Principles**: Write down transferable rules for future decisions
5. **Track Patterns**: Review your decision portfolio quarterly to identify trends
